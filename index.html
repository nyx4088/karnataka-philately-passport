FAQ

How do I mark a place visited?
Visit a post office, tap its tile in Passport, add a photo of your stamp — it's marked visited automatically, with the visit date saved. You'll see "Marked visited." with an Undo button if you want to revert. Prefer no photo? Use Mark visited without photo on the office card.
Where are my photos stored?
On this device only. Photos are kept in on-device storage, never uploaded to any server. They are also not included if you export your progress as a JSON backup — the backup carries your visited/planned lists only.
What do the passport tile colors mean?
Green with a ✓ = visited (stamped). Dashed amber = planned (in your Postbox). Plain = not visited yet. The number is the booklet serial; the district postmark shows at a glance where it belongs.
What does "Approximate location" mean?
The pin is placed at the town centre, not the exact office building. Use the Directions button (which searches the post office by name and PIN) rather than the pin alone when you're close.
How do I get directions?
Tap any pin or tile, then Directions — it opens Google Maps searching the post office by name and PIN. Directions to map pin navigates to the exact pinned point instead. "Getting there" names the nearest major transit town as a hint only; there is no live transport data and no fares.
Is my progress backed up anywhere?
Only if you back it up: use Export progress under Share & backup to save a JSON file, and Import to restore it. Photos are not part of the backup. Clearing browser data erases everything unless you exported.
What are Circuits?
Suggested region-wise routes (North, Coastal, South, etc.) to plan multi-stop stamp runs. They're neutral suggestions — start wherever you are.
Why is only Karnataka on the map?
That's the whole quest — the passport covers Karnataka's 100 post offices only. Everything outside the state is hidden by design.

About this data

Locations are the 100 post offices from the Karnataka Philately Passport booklet (₹600, issued October 2026 by the Department of Posts, Karnataka Circle). Coordinates were geocoded per post office — 34 hand-verified to the actual office, 3 near-verified, the rest town-level approximations labelled "Approximate location" on their cards; the Directions button searches the post office on Google Maps, and "Directions to map pin" navigates to the exact pinned point.

Pictorial-cancellation motifs and introduction dates come from the public permanent pictorial cancellation registry. Where no registry entry matched the booklet's post office, the motif section is omitted — check your booklet on the ground.

"Getting there" names the nearest major transit town as an indicative hint only — use Directions for live routing. Nothing here is live transport data and there are no fares.

Privacy

Everything — your visited and planned lists, theme choice, and location (used only when you tap "Near me") — stays in this browser's local storage on your device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Clearing browser data erases your progress unless you exported a backup.
