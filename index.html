About this data

Locations are the 100 post offices from the Karnataka Philately Passport booklet (₹600, issued October 2026 by the Department of Posts, Karnataka Circle). Coordinates were geocoded per post office — most resolve to the actual office, a few are town-level approximations; the Directions button always navigates to the exact pinned point.

Pictorial-cancellation motifs and introduction dates come from the public permanent pictorial cancellation registry. Where no registry entry matched the booklet's post office, the motif section is omitted — check your booklet on the ground.

"Getting there" names the nearest major transit town as an indicative hint only — use Directions for live routing. Nothing here is live transport data and there are no fares.

Privacy

Everything — your visited and planned lists, theme choice, and location (used only when you tap "Near me") — stays in this browser's local storage on your device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Clearing browser data erases your progress unless you exported a backup.
